记账本 (Ledger)

A simple personal expense tracker that works offline and syncs to your own Google Drive.

打开记账本 / Open the app

这是什么 / What it is

记账本是一个个人记账网页应用：记录每一笔收入和支出，按分类和账户统计，支持离线使用，并可安装到手机、平板和电脑。

Ledger is a personal finance web app for recording income and expenses by category and account. It works offline and can be installed on phones, tablets and computers.

Google Drive 同步 / Google Drive sync

如果你选择连接 Google 账号，应用只会使用 drive.file 权限，在你自己的 Google Drive 里创建并读写一个名为“记账本数据.json”的文件，用来在你的多台设备之间同步账本。应用无法访问你 Drive 里的其他文件。

If you connect your Google account, the app uses only the drive.file scope to create and read a single file named “记账本数据.json” in your own Google Drive, to sync your ledger between your devices. It cannot access any other files in your Drive.
