中文

我们收集什么。记账本不设服务器，也不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何数据。你记录的账目只保存在你自己设备的浏览器里。

Google 用户数据。如果你主动点击“连接并同步”，应用会请求 Google Drive 的 https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file 权限。该权限只允许应用访问它自己创建的文件。应用用它在你的 Google Drive 中创建并读写一个名为“记账本数据.json”的文件，内容是你的账目数据，仅用于在你自己的设备之间同步。

数据如何使用。这些数据只在你的设备和你自己的 Google Drive 之间传输，不会发送给开发者或任何第三方，也不用于广告、分析或训练任何模型。应用对 Google 用户数据的使用，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的有限使用要求。

如何撤销与删除。你可以随时在应用设置里点“断开连接”，也可以在 Google 账号权限页面撤销授权，并在你的 Google Drive 中删除“记账本数据.json”。清除浏览器网站数据会删除本机保存的账目。

English

What we collect. Ledger has no server and does not collect, upload or sell any of your data. The entries you record are stored only in your own device's browser.

Google user data. If you choose “Connect and sync”, the app requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which only allows access to files the app itself created. The app uses it to create and read one file named “记账本数据.json” in your Google Drive, containing your ledger data, solely to sync it between your own devices.

How data is used. Data moves only between your devices and your own Google Drive. It is never sent to the developer or any third party, and is not used for advertising, analytics or training any model. The app's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking and deleting. You can click “Disconnect” in the app settings at any time, revoke access on your Google Account permissions page, and delete the file “记账本数据.json” from your Google Drive. Clearing your browser's site data removes the entries stored on that device.
